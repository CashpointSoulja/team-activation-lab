Team Activation Lab

Independent concept · synthetic data · not an official TryHackMe tool

Buyer-to-first-value, with a measured experiment

Get a trial team to its first useful lab, then decide honestly whether it worked.

A manager picks one goal, gets a seven-day proof plan and moves two learners through a first meaningful lab. The growth view then checks whether that journey beat the ordinary signup flow, and says no decision when the data cannot support one.

Everything here is synthetic. Invitations are never sent, labs are illustrative, there is no TryHackMe account connection, and finishing a first lab is not a claim of security readiness. The activation definition is a hypothesis that needs real research.
