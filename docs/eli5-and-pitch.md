# ELI5 and 30-second pitch (as supplied)

## Team Activation Lab, in plain English

A company tries cyber training, but signing up does not mean the team gets useful practice. This concept helps the manager pick one goal, make a short proof plan and get two people through a first useful lab.

The second half asks whether that journey is worth shipping. It compares fake accounts with the ordinary journey against fake accounts with the goal-led plan. It shows what happened, who was counted and whether the data is good enough. If the sample is too small or tracking is broken, it says "no decision" instead of pretending there is a winner.

This is not another training dashboard. It is a buyer-to-first-value journey with a measured experiment attached. TryHackMe's Growth PM role calls for onboarding, commercial collaboration and experiments, so the product demonstrates all three in one small working loop.

Everything is synthetic. Invitations do not send, training content is illustrative and there is no TryHackMe account connection. First lab completion is not a claim of security readiness. The proposed activation definition needs real research before production.

## 30-second pitch

"I built Team Activation Lab for the B2B Growth PM role. A manager chooses a team goal, sees a seven-day proof plan and tests getting a first learner cohort to value. The growth view traces the account funnel, checks the experiment setup and exports a decision. It also refuses to call a winner when the data is bad. It's fake data, but a real product and measurement loop."
