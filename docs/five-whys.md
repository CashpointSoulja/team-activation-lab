# 5 Whys (research hypothesis, not a diagnosis)

This chain is a hypothesis to test in discovery. It is not a claim about TryHackMe.

1. **Symptom (hypothesised):** an evaluation does not become team engagement.
2. **Why?** The invited cohort never gets a clear next step.
3. **Why?** The manager starts from a broad catalogue rather than a goal.
4. **Why?** The purchase/evaluation flow and the training plan are separate.
5. **Why?** Initial value is described in marketing but not operationalised in product.
6. **Why?** The buyer's job (justify the purchase) and the learner's job (learn something) differ, and nothing connects them in week one.

**First falsifier:** buyers already get a clear plan and the stall is procurement, not activation. If discovery shows that, this concept is the wrong bet (see [opportunity-tree.md](opportunity-tree.md)).
