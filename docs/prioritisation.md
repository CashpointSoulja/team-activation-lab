# Prioritisation rationale

No RICE numbers are given because reach and impact are unknown, and fabricated scores would look precise without being true.

| Option | Bounded? | Testable in one cycle? | Maps to role? | Risk if wrong | Call |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 Goal-led proof plan | Yes, one screen + plan template | Yes, account-level A/B | Onboarding, activation, Sales/CS handoff | Low: rollback is a flag | **Build first** |
| Landing-page conversion | Yes | Yes | Marketing collaboration | Moves signups, which may be vanity | Later |
| Procurement checklist (C1) | Yes | Hard to A/B (low volume, long cycle) | Enterprise buyer enablement | Low | After discovery |
| Expansion nudges (D2) | Yes | Yes | Monetisation | Premature before first value exists | Later |

**Why first-value clarity before monetisation:** an upsell to a team that never activated has nothing to expand. It is also the narrowest change that touches Product, Sales and CS together.

**Change direction if** discovery says procurement or seat economics is the bottleneck (see [five-whys.md](five-whys.md) falsifier).
