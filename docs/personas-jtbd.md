# Personas and jobs to be done

These are working personas from the supplied PRD. They are **not** based on interviews; none have been conducted.

## Primary: security manager evaluating team training
- **Situation:** has signed up or is trialling for a team of 5–50; owns a goal like onboarding analysts.
- **Job:** "When I'm evaluating training, give me a concrete proof plan for my team's goal so I can judge value before a broader rollout."
- **Success looks like:** two people did something useful this week and I can show it.
- **Anxieties:** wasting the team's time; picking the wrong content; looking unprepared to leadership.
- **What the concept gives them:** goal choice → 7-day plan → cohort invite (simulated) → first-value checklist.

## Secondary: Growth PM
- **Job:** "When an account stalls, distinguish journey friction from bad instrumentation and decide what to test next."
- **Success:** a decision they can defend, including "we can't tell yet".
- **What the concept gives them:** validation and quarantine, funnel with denominators, account timeline, assignment check, quality gates, decision export.

## Supporting: Sales / Customer Success
- **Job:** "When helping an evaluation, see the agreed goal, invited cohort and observed progress without replacing the training manager's dashboard."
- **What the concept gives them:** stall step per account and a suggested (illustrative) handoff play. Nothing is sent.

## Anti-persona
- An individual learner looking for content. This is not a learning product.
