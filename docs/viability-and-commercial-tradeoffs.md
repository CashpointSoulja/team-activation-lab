# Viability and commercial trade-offs

No ROI, revenue or uplift figures are given; none are known.

## When this is worth building
Only if (1) buyer-to-team activation is a real source of evaluation loss, and (2) an early team signal like `activated_7d` predicts retention or paid conversion. Both are unvalidated.

## Trade-offs
| Choice | Gain | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Goal-led plan in product vs CS-led kickoff | Scales to self-serve; measurable | Less tailored than a human call; CS may already do this |
| Two-learner threshold | Team signal, not one keen admin | Harder for very small teams to hit |
| 7-day window | Fast learning cycles | May undercount slow-starting enterprises |
| Account-level randomisation | Correct unit, no contamination | Needs more accounts than learner-level; B2B volumes may be small, so tests take longer |
| Ship only on interval > 0 and ≥ 5 pp | Fewer false wins | Small real effects may be missed or take longer |

## Commercial collaboration
- **Sales:** sees the goal and stall step for accounts in evaluation; can offer help at the right step.
- **CS:** suggested handoff plays per stall step; proof summary for the buyer's leadership (V2).
- **Marketing:** the goal list doubles as messaging test material for landing pages.

## Sample-size reality check
At a 17% baseline, detecting +5 pp with 80% power at 95% needs roughly 900–1,000 accounts per arm (normal approximation). If B2B evaluation volume is lower, the realistic options are a longer test, a larger minimum effect, or a staged rollout with qualitative evidence. This is arithmetic, not a forecast.
