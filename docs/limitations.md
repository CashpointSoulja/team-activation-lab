# Honest limitations

- **Synthetic only.** Every number is generated. Scenario A's effect is planted by a generator parameter.
- **No research.** Zero interviews. Personas and the 5 Whys are hypotheses.
- **Activation definition is unvalidated.** It may not predict retention or paid conversion.
- **Not a readiness measure.** Finishing a first lab says nothing about security capability.
- **No integrations.** No TryHackMe account, API, CRM or email. Invitations never send.
- **Local state only.** Not shared, authenticated or durable; large uploads may not persist.
- **Simple statistics.** Two-arm binary outcomes, unadjusted; no covariates, no sequential testing, no multiple-comparison control. Gates catch obvious faults, not subtle bias.
- **Preview always shows treatment.** The control journey is represented only in data.
- **Illustrative content.** Goals, plans and lab titles are placeholders, not TryHackMe material.
- **Not affiliated.** Independent concept by Ayo Ahmed; TryHackMe name and logo belong to TryHackMe.
